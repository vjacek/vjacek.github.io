Every once in a while, Dad would say something about there being Train Guys and Rocket Guys, implying, but not directly saying, that they must be so very different from each other, and that it was important to know where you stood. Sometimes, it was even important to know if someone else was a Train Guy or a Rocket Guy too.

"Well, there are Train Guys, and there are Rocket Guys."

There was never a lot of explanation about the merits, demerits, or differences between Trains and Rockets, almost as if it was obvious. At the time, it never was obvious, but now I suspect that having to figure it out for yourself was also part of the lesson. Wisdom that wasn't easy to understand but only earned by your own merit of thought was fairly common for us to receive. Even upon the repeated questioning of 3 inquisitive sons, the only forthcoming explanation was that there indeed were both Train Guys and Rocket Guys.

"Hey, there _are_ Train Guys, _and_ Rocket Guys."

Trains are of course huge machines, but so are Rockets, even more so. They're both complex and take hard work to operate, and seeing either one in your normal day is special. They both take enormous quantities of fuel, and both represent adventure for an ambitious traveler. Trains and Rockets are both marvels of engineering worthy of study. Trains are old and Rockets newer, but representing the past and future was just too simplistic to be the full story. They were each the epitome of engineering and technology of their time, but again, time alone wouldn't cut it.

I might have an idea of what makes Train Guys and Rocket Guys.

Train Guys are reliabile, meeting the demands of the world with straightforward competence through elimination of risk. They design, build, and live in ways that stay safe.

Rocket Guys are explorers, trying to capitalize on the new and novel by accepting risk and even defeat if it can be worth the achievement at the end. They design, build, and live in ways that push boundaries.

I think it's _supposed_ to be hard to decide between them; there's no easy winner for all scenarios. Both Train Guys and Rocket Guys will have their share of successes and failures. Choosing Train or Rocket can help influence the velocity, _magnitude and direction_, of your outcomes.

I've come to the opinion that the superpower here is not in making a choice for always, but in recognizing what the current situation demands and warrants, then acting accordingly. The next time you're standing on the edge of decision, choose and commit decisively, because afterall -

"There are Train Guys and there are Rocket Guys."
